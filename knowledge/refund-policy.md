# Refund Policy

## Eligibility window

Charges are eligible for a full refund if the refund request is made within
14 days of the charge date. This applies to both monthly and annual billing
cycles — for annual plans, the 14-day window starts from the most recent
renewal charge, not the original signup date.

## Refunds after cancellation

If you cancel your subscription but are charged again before the
cancellation takes effect (for example, a renewal that processed moments
before you cancelled), that charge is refunded automatically within 5
business days. You do not need to contact support for this case. See
`cancellation-policy.md` for how cancellation timing works.

## Duplicate or billing-error charges

Duplicate charges caused by a billing system error are refunded in full
regardless of the 14-day window, as soon as the duplicate is confirmed.
Contact support with the charge dates and amounts so the team can verify
the duplicate before issuing the refund.

## What is not refundable

Charges older than 14 days are not refundable, except for the duplicate or
billing-error case above. Partial-month usage is not prorated for refund
purposes — refunds are issued for the full charge amount or not at all.

## How to request a refund

Email support with your account email and the date of the charge you're
disputing. Most requests are resolved within 1 business day; approved
refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-10 business
days, depending on your bank.
