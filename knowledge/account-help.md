# Account Help

## Resetting your password

Go to the login page and click "Forgot password." Enter your account
email and a reset link is sent immediately — it expires after 1 hour.
If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check spam and
confirm you're using the email address your account was created with.

## Getting locked out

After 5 failed login attempts, the account is temporarily locked for 30
minutes as a security measure. You can also reset your password
immediately to unlock the account right away instead of waiting.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA can be enabled under Settings > Security. It uses any standard
authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.). When you enable
2FA, you're given 10 single-use recovery codes — store these somewhere
safe, since losing both your authenticator app and your recovery codes
means account recovery requires manual identity verification with
support.

## Changing your account email

Settings > Account > Email. A confirmation link is sent to the new
address; the change only takes effect once that link is clicked, so your
old email keeps working until then.

## "I can't log in" checklist

1. Confirm you're using the exact email your account was created with.
2. Clear your browser cache or try an incognito/private window.
3. If your company uses SSO, log in through your company's SSO portal
   instead of the regular email/password form.
4. If none of the above works, use "Forgot password" to reset your
   credentials rather than repeatedly retrying, to avoid the 5-attempt
   lockout.
