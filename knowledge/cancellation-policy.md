# Cancellation Policy

## How to cancel

Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription and confirm. Cancellation
takes effect immediately in the sense that no future charges are
scheduled, but your plan features remain active until the end of the
current billing period.

## What happens to your access

You keep full access to your current plan (Starter, Pro, or Business —
see `subscription-plans.md`) until the end of the billing period you've
already paid for. After that date, the account moves to a read-only,
free-tier state: you can log in and view past tickets, but shared inbox
automation rules and integrations are paused.

## What happens to your data

Your account data (tickets, automation rules, integration connections) is
retained for 30 days after the billing period ends, in case you want to
reactivate. After 30 days of inactivity on a cancelled account, the data
is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## Refunds on cancellation

Cancelling does not automatically trigger a refund for the current billing
period — you keep access through the period you already paid for. If you
were charged again after cancelling, see the "Refunds after cancellation"
section in `refund-policy.md`.

## Reactivating

You can reactivate any time before the 30-day data retention window ends
by resubscribing to any plan from Settings > Billing. Reactivating after
that window means starting with a fresh account.
