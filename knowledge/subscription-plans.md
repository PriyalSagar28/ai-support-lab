# Subscription Plans

## Starter — $9/month

For individuals getting started. Includes 1 user seat, up to 500 support
tickets per month, and email support with a 1 business day response time.
No automation rules or shared inbox.

## Pro — $29/month

For small support teams. Includes up to 5 user seats, unlimited tickets,
a shared inbox with automation rules, and priority support with same-day
response. This is the most popular plan.

## Business — $79/month

For growing teams that need more control. Includes up to 20 user seats,
everything in Pro, advanced automation rules, single sign-on (SSO), and a
dedicated account manager.

## Annual billing

Paying annually instead of monthly gives a 20% discount on any plan. The
discount is applied automatically at checkout when you select "Bill
annually."

## Changing plans

Upgrading takes effect immediately and you're charged a prorated amount
for the rest of the current billing period. Downgrading takes effect at
the start of your next billing period — you keep your current plan's
features until then, and there is no partial refund for downgrading
mid-period.

## Seat limits

If your team grows beyond your plan's seat limit, you'll be prompted to
upgrade before adding the next user. Removing a user frees up a seat
immediately.
