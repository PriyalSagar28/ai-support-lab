# Billing FAQ

## When am I charged?

Monthly plans are charged on the same day of the month as your original
signup date. Annual plans are charged once a year on your renewal date.
You can see your next charge date under Settings > Billing.

## Why was I charged twice this month?

This is almost always one of two things: a plan upgrade (which charges a
prorated amount in addition to your regular renewal) or a genuine billing
error. Check Settings > Billing > Invoice History first — if you see two
charges of the same amount on the same day with no plan change, that's a
duplicate charge. See `refund-policy.md` for how duplicate charges are
refunded.

## What payment methods are accepted?

Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal.
Bank transfer is available on the Business plan only, arranged directly
with support.

## How do I update my card on file?

Settings > Billing > Payment Method > Update. The new card is used
starting with your next scheduled charge; it does not retroactively
affect past charges.

## Can I get an invoice or receipt?

Yes. Every charge generates an invoice automatically, available under
Settings > Billing > Invoice History as a downloadable PDF.

## What happens if a payment fails?

A failed payment is retried up to 3 times over 7 days. You'll get an
email after each failed attempt. If all 3 retries fail, the account is
suspended until a valid payment method is added — your data is not
deleted at this stage, only access is paused.
